Exvius Revival Desktop: quick start
===================================

You need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), your own FINAL FANTASY BRAVE EXVIUS 9.1.0 APK (Android,
Global version; .apk, .xapk or .apks), and about 31 GB of free disk space (15 GB once set up).

1. Unpack this folder somewhere with room, for example C:\Games\ExviusRevivalDesktop.
   Keep the folder together.
2. Double-click "Exvius Revival Desktop.exe".
   If Windows says the program is from an unknown publisher: More info, then Run anyway.
3. Click "Choose APK or XAPK" and pick your APK. The game is copied out of it into this folder.
4. Click "Download game data" (14.6 GB). You can close the window and carry on later.
5. Click "Play".

The first time: the title screen shows some labels as codes (BTN_INFO). Tap the screen, choose
"None" for account linking and accept the download prompt. The title screen then reads normally.
Tap to start.

Playing: click and drag are touch, the mouse wheel scrolls. The bar above the game has Back
(also Esc), volume (F9 and F10), a picture button (F12) and full screen (F11). Drag the bar to
move the window.

Your save is in the "save" folder here. To remove the program, delete this folder; nothing is
installed anywhere else.

Everything else is in README.txt.
